# Code of Conduct

Contributors must communicate respectfully, critique ideas rather than people,
and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or disclosure of private
information.

Maintainers may remove content or participation that undermines a safe,
constructive project.

For a private report, use the repository's
[private reporting form](https://github.com/Jason-Doyle/ContentContractEngine/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `Conduct report`. Do not include private information
that is not needed to investigate the report.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality where
possible, and take proportionate action based on the available evidence.
